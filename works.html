Works in the house

Everything on the walls is a reproduction, printed full size on canvas in Almaty in 2013 and replaced in 2022 after nine damp winters. The house is unheated from October to May. The originals, 41 paintings and about 120 drawings, are at the Palace of Art.

Original here: his easel, chair, paint box and palette knife, the saddle, three letters to his brother (1911–1915), the 1930 schoolbooks, and the house itself.

Yusup Ayyubov House-Museum · upper Besh-Tash valley, Talas District · keeper Saltanat Jumabekova · beshtash@korkom.org · in season +996-39-5-41-02-33 (ranger post)
