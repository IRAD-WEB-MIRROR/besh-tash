Visit

Season1 June – 30 September. Wednesday to Sunday, 10:00–17:00.
Tickets100 som; children, students and pensioners 30 som; foreigners 300 som. Cash only. Free on 24 August.
ParkThe house is inside Besh-Tash National Park: pay the park fee at the ranger post.
Getting thereFrom Talas by the Besh-Tash road to the ranger post (paved, about 1 h 30 by car). Then 7 km of track: on foot (about 2 h 30 up), on horseback with the Akjele families at the post (book the day before), or by 4×4 in dry weather.
Altitude2,400 m. Bring warm clothes even in July.
GroupsUp to 25, Wednesdays and Thursdays, by arrangement.
FoodNone at the house. Kymyz and bread at the post in July and August.
PhoneNo signal at the house. Write to beshtash@korkom.org or ring the ranger post.

Out of season the track is under snow. See the paintings at the Palace of Art, Iyık Janyl.
